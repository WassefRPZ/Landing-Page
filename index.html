Everything you need to play together

BoardGame Hub brings together everything you need to organize, discover, and enjoy your board game nights.

Event Management

Create and join board game nights near you. Search by city or date, manage your attendance and never miss a game night again.

Games Catalog

Browse a full catalog of board games. Add your favorites to your profile and find events around the games you love.

Social & Friends

Connect with other players, add them as friends, leave comments on events and build your own network of gamers.

About

The story behind the project

It all started from a simple frustration: organizing a board game night with friends was always a hassle. Endless WhatsApp groups, improvised Google Sheets, forgotten RSVPs… We wanted something simple, social, and built for gamers.

That's how BoardGame Hub was born — a platform made by players, for players. This project was developed from March to June 2025 as part of the Holberton School Portfolio Project program.
